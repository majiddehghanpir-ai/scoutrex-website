What JobRex does for you

More than job search. Your complete career portal.

JobRex gives you every tool you need: finding the right roles, taking skill assessments, scheduling interviews, and tracking your entire pipeline from one dashboard.

AI-Powered Profile & CV Import

Import your CV and let AI extract your skills, experience, and profile automatically. Build a living career profile that goes beyond keywords, capturing your trajectory, working style, and real potential.

Intelligent Job Matching

AI matches you on trajectory, culture fit, and growth potential, not just job titles. Browse open roles, filter by location, salary, and type, and apply directly with your JobRex profile.

Skill Assessments

Take role-specific skill tests that let you prove what you can actually do, not just what your CV says. Scores can be shared with hiring teams when they are relevant to an application.

AI Interview Calendar & Scheduling

Set your availability once and AI helps match it against the hiring team's open slots. Propose alternative times, accept or decline, and track every scheduled interview from your JobRex dashboard.

Direct Messaging with Companies

Communicate directly with recruiters inside the platform. Every message is attached to your application so context is always there and nothing gets lost in an email inbox.

Application Tracker & Pipeline View

See every application, every stage, and every score in one dashboard. Know exactly where you stand, from shortlisted to hired, with real-time status updates and notification alerts.

The Science

Grounded in assessment science, not black-box scoring

ScoutRex is built on established methods for job-related aptitude, designed to validate scoring against real-world outcomes rather than keywords.

DIN 33430-aligned assessment

Assessments are designed around DIN 33430, the German standard for job-related aptitude diagnostics: objectivity, reliability, validity and fairness by design.

Causal, outcome-driven intelligence

A causal-inference layer is built to connect pre-hire signals with post-hire performance, so the engine learns which signals actually predict success.

Backed by university research

Methodology is developed in close connection with the Institute of Labour Economics at Leibniz University Hannover, with academic mentorship from Prof. Dr. Patrick Puhani.

Building intelligent systems that make career transitions and talent discovery faster, fairer, and more human, powered by AI.
