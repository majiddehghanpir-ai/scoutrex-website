Platform Capabilities

Everything you need to hire right. All in one platform.

HireRex isn't just a screening tool. It's a complete hiring operating system. From the first application to the signed offer, every step is powered by AI.

🧪

AI-Generated Assessments

HireRex creates job-specific technical tests tailored to each role's exact requirements in seconds. Build a custom Question Library or let AI generate role-matched tests on demand, for every position.

⚡

Instant AI Scoring & Ranking

Candidates are scored automatically with detailed skill breakdowns, weighted scoring, and structured reports. From hundreds of applicants to a ranked shortlist, with no manual review at any stage.

📅

AI Interview Calendar & Scheduling

AI reads your team's availability and the candidate's proposed slots, picks the best match, and sends confirmed invitations automatically. Counter-proposals, rescheduling, and waitlist promotion all handled without a single back-and-forth email.

📁

Document Collection & AI Verification

Define required documents per role: certificates, compliance files, portfolios. Candidates upload them in-portal and AI evaluates each submission, flagging missing or insufficient items instantly.

💬

Built-in Messaging & Email Outreach

Message candidates directly inside HireRex with no external email threads needed. Send personalised outreach campaigns, track delivery, and keep every conversation attached to the candidate's profile and pipeline stage.

🤖

Automated Candidate Routing

Top candidates are routed to interviews automatically. AI analyses job requirements, extracts skills from JDs, and matches candidates with precise fit scores. Your team only sees who is genuinely worth their time.

📊

Structured Hiring Reports

Every candidate gets an AI-explained score with a detailed breakdown. Hiring decisions are fast, fair, and fully defensible. All data is exportable to XLSX or CSV for your own reporting workflow.

📈

Analytics & Pipeline Intelligence

Pipeline analytics, time-to-hire, assessment quality scores, team performance dashboards, and AI feedback loops, giving you the data to continuously improve how your team hires.

Candidate CRM

Your entire candidate relationship. One place.

HireRex includes a built-in CRM so your team never loses context on a candidate, from first contact to final decision and beyond. No separate tool needed.

🗒️
Recruiter Notes & Activity Log
Log notes on any candidate at any stage. Every interaction is time-stamped and visible to your whole team, creating a complete, searchable history for every person in your pipeline.
📧
Personalised Outreach Campaigns
Send targeted email sequences to candidates directly from HireRex. Track opens and replies in the same pipeline view where you see their test scores and application status.
👥
Team Collaboration & Permissions
Invite recruiters, assign granular roles and permissions, and collaborate on hiring decisions in real time. Each team member sees exactly what their role permits and nothing more.
🏢
Multi-Company Management
Recruiting agencies and talent ops teams can manage multiple companies under one platform, each with their own branding, pipelines, and recruiter teams.
