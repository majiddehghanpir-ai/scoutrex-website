How It Works

From first application to signed offer. End to end.

Five automated steps that replace your entire hiring stack: assessment, scoring, scheduling, document collection, and CRM. No manual review required at any stage.

01

Post Role & Import Candidates

Create a job position, define requirements in plain language, and connect your existing ATS or import CVs directly. HireRex picks up from there with no disruption to your current workflow.

02

AI Generates Tailored Assessments

Job-specific tests are created in seconds from your Question Library or generated fresh by AI, tailored to the exact skills, seniority, and role requirements you defined.

03

Candidates Test, Upload Docs & Score

Candidates complete assessments, submit required documents, and are scored automatically. Weighted AI scoring produces a ranked shortlist with full explanations. No manual review needed.

04

AI Schedules Interviews Automatically

Top candidates are invited to interview based on your team's availability calendar. Rescheduling, counter-proposals, and waitlist promotion all happen without anyone on your team lifting a finger.

05

CRM, Outreach & Team Collaboration

Add recruiter notes, send personalised outreach, message candidates in-app, and collaborate with your team. Everything is logged to the candidate's CRM profile, all inside one platform.
