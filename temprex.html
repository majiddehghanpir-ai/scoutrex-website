Skills intelligence, built for the full talent lifecycle

Your workforce's verified skills. In one place.

TalentRex continuously verifies skills across your workforce — grounded in real work evidence, not self-reports. Managers get defensible performance data. Employees get transparent growth tracking. The whole team benefits from better internal mobility matches.

1

Verify skills continuously — from real evidence

TalentRex uses adaptive assessments, work samples, and manager input to build a verified skills profile for every employee. Longitudinal tracking shows growth over time, with audit-ready trails for defensible decisions.

2

Manage performance with structured work plans

Work Planner and Performance Analyzer agents track task completion, output quality, timeliness, and collaboration. AI-generated reports keep managers and HR informed with objective data — not gut feel.

3

Match employees to internal opportunities

When a role opens internally, TalentRex matches it against the verified skills, career goals, and performance data of your entire workforce. Internal mobility that's fair, fast, and grounded in evidence.

What TalentRex delivers

The full talent lifecycle, powered by verified skills.

From verifying what your team actually knows, to managing performance with evidence, to matching employees to internal opportunities — TalentRex covers the talent lifecycle that enterprise HR tools miss.

✅

Skills Verification Dashboard

Continuously verify skills across your entire workforce using adaptive assessments, real work evidence, and manager input. Longitudinal skill growth tracking, per-role benchmarks, and audit-ready trails for every decision — built for DACH regulatory defensibility.

📊

AI Performance Management

Work Planner and Performance Analyzer agents evaluate output quality, timeliness, productivity, reliability, and collaboration. AI-generated performance reports give managers structured, objective data — not gut feel — backed by defensible audit trails.

🗂️

Structured Work Planning

Assign tasks, set milestones, and balance workloads across your team with TalentRex's Work Planner Agent. Track deliverables, flag blockers, and keep every manager and employee aligned — without spreadsheets.

🔄

Internal Mobility Matching (v2)

When a role opens internally, TalentRex matches it against verified skills, career goals, and performance data across your entire workforce. Fair, evidence-based internal mobility that retains your best people and fills roles faster.

📚

LearnRex Skill Gap Closing

When TalentRex identifies a skill gap, it automatically triggers a targeted LearnRex course. When the employee completes the course, TalentRex re-verifies the skill. A closed loop from gap detection to verified mastery.

🔗

Deep Ecosystem Integration

TalentRex connects natively with HireRex (verified candidate skills carry into onboarding), LearnRex (gap-to-course-to-verification loop), Personio, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, and 50+ HRIS platforms via Merge.dev.
