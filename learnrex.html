AI-powered learning

Every gap has a course. Every course has a certificate.

LearnRex isn't a generic course platform. Every recommendation is tied to a real skill gap in your profile, a test you didn't pass, or a role you're targeting.

1

AI identifies your specific gap

When you fail a skill test in JobRex, or a weakness is flagged in TempRex, LearnRex is notified automatically and queues the right course, not a random catalogue.

2

Learn at your pace. Quiz at the end.

Structured lessons, real examples, and a final assessment. Pass the quiz and you've earned it. The result is reflected back in your JobRex profile automatically.

3

Earn a verified certificate

Every completed course comes with a LearnRex certificate that companies can verify. Show employers real proof of your skills, not just a line on a CV.

What LearnRex offers

Three ways LearnRex works for you.

Whether you're a job seeker closing a gap, a Temp assigned training, or a company upskilling new hires, LearnRex has a learning path for it.

❌

Failed a Test? Get the Fix.

When a candidate doesn't pass a skill assessment in JobRex, LearnRex immediately recommends the targeted course for that topic. No more losing roles for the same gap twice.

📈

Self-Directed Upskilling

Browse courses by role, industry, or skill area. Build the knowledge that makes you competitive for the jobs you actually want, on your own schedule.

🏢

Company-Assigned Training

During a Probezeit managed by TempRex, the host company can assign specific LearnRex courses to their Temp directly. Progress is tracked and reported back automatically.

🏆

Verified Certificates

Finish a course, pass the quiz, earn a certificate issued by ScoutRex LearnRex. Certificates are linked to your JobRex profile so companies see verified proof of skills, not self-claims.

🤖

AI Learning Recommendations

Your learning path adapts based on the roles you're applying for, the tests you take, and the feedback from assessments. LearnRex knows what you need before you have to search for it.

🔗

Fully Integrated Across Products

JobRex feeds test results. TempRex assigns company training. HireRex sees certificate completions. LearnRex is the shared learning layer that connects the entire ScoutRex ecosystem.
